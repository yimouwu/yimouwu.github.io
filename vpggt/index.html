VPGGT is an embodied-perception foundation model that combines images with optional camera parameters and depth observations to predict camera parameters, depth maps, and pointmaps with a shared metric scale. By leveraging geometric priors supplied by upstream sensing or estimation, the model circumvents the direct processing of raw sensor streams.

Abstract

TL;DR VPGGT combines noisy pose-prior training with a parameter-free Prior Residual Connection (PRC) to improve geometric-prior use in feed-forward embodied perception, and adds Metric Global Attention to recover a shared physical scale.

Embodied systems need geometric perception that exploits available observations beyond images alone. Recent feed-forward 3D models incorporate geometric priors, including camera poses, intrinsics, and depth. However, handling noisy poses, preserving accurate priors, and recovering physical scale require more than simply accepting these inputs. We introduce the Vision-Prior Geometry Grounded Transformer (VPGGT), a VGGT-based framework that extends OmniVGGT for prior-aware embodied perception.

We formulate sensor-motivated pose corruptions from ground-truth trajectories for training and introduce a parameter-free Prior Residual Connection (PRC) to mitigate prior dilution, where predictions are less accurate than their supplied pose priors. Our noise formulation targets camera poses; supplied intrinsics and depth receive no additional corruption. We further introduce Metric Global Attention, which conditions a global scale token on available pose and depth scales and predicts a shared metric scaling factor for the geometric outputs.

Experiments across four datasets show that PRC improves translation-direction accuracy and joint pose AUC over a matched training baseline when camera priors are provided for all views, under both exact and corrupted poses. These results support explicit prior access during refinement as a useful addition to feature-level conditioning.

Sensor-motivated pose corruptions synthesized from ground-truth trajectories — accumulated drift and independent (tracker-style) perturbations — move prior-conditioned training beyond the common exact-prior assumption. Only camera poses are corrupted; intrinsics and depth remain clean.
  • 02 Prior Residual Connection (PRC). A parameter-free mechanism that places scale-aligned priors into the camera head's refinement state, so that subsequent residual updates correct a supplied prior rather than an estimated state — directly targeting prior dilution.
  • 03 Metric Global Attention (MGA). A global scale token conditioned on available pose and depth scale statistics predicts a single shared metric factor that converts translations, depths, and points to physical units — using learned placeholders when scale observations are absent.

The Problem: Prior Dilution

We use prior to denote a supplied geometric estimate. For a higher-is-better pose metric $M$, let $M_{\mathrm{prior}}$ and $M_{\mathrm{out}}$ be the scores of the input prior and the model prediction on the same eligible image pairs. We define their gap as $d_M = M_{\mathrm{prior}} - M_{\mathrm{out}}$ and refer to $d_M > 0$ as prior dilution — the model produces camera estimates less accurate than the priors it was given, even when those priors are exact.

When the prior outperforms the matched no-camera-prior reference $M_0$, closure of the baseline-to-prior gap is $\kappa_M = \tfrac{M_{\mathrm{out}} - M_0}{M_{\mathrm{prior}} - M_0}$, with dilution $\mathcal{D}_M = 1 - \kappa_M$.

A state reset recovers the exact-prior score, but each subsequent refinement update reduces AUC@30° — direct access to a prior state alone does not ensure the existing refinement operator preserves its accuracy.

This motivates training the model with PRC so that the refinement operator learns corrections that respect, rather than dilute, informative priors.

Method

Given images $\mathcal{I} = \{I_i\}_{i=1}^{N}$ and optional camera observations $\mathcal{C}$ and masked depth observations $\mathcal{D}$, VPGGT predicts a shared positive scale $\hat{s}$ together with per-view camera parameters, depth maps, and pointmaps. It builds on an OmniVGGT backbone (24 alternating frame/global attention pairs, token dimension 1024) and retains the inherited camera, depth, and pointmap heads.

[ architecture figure — coming soon ]
Overview of VPGGT. Images and optional camera or depth priors are encoded and processed by a shared alternating-attention backbone. PRC places available camera priors in the camera head's refinement state after its initial prediction. A Scale Encoder conditions a global token on available metric-scale statistics, using learned placeholders when they are absent. The camera, depth, and pointmap heads predict geometry, and a scale head supplies a shared factor that converts translations, depths, and points to metric units.

Prior Residual Connection (PRC)

The camera head already refines predictions through residual updates. PRC changes the reference for these updates from an estimated camera state to the supplied prior wherever one is available, following the residual-learning principle of learning corrections to a reference.

After recording the initial prediction $\mathbf{r}_i^0$, PRC resets the state to the scale-aligned prior, $\mathbf{u}_i^0 = \mathrm{sg}[\,m_i \mathbf{a}_i + (1-m_i)\mathbf{r}_i^0\,]$, where $m_i$ indicates prior availability and $\mathrm{sg}$ denotes stop-gradient. Remaining iterations apply the original refinement operator. PRC adds no parameters and leaves the computation unchanged when camera priors are absent.

Metric Global Attention (MGA)

MGA retains the physical-scale information removed when normalizing geometric priors. A shared encoder maps the log scale and coverage of each available modality (camera, depth) to a feature, using learned modality-specific placeholders when a scale is missing. The resulting scale token is appended to the multi-view sequence for global attention, bypasses frame attention, and finally predicts a positive factor $\hat{s} = \exp\!\big(h(\mathrm{LN}(\mathbf{t}_s^L))\big)$ that scales depths, translations, and pointmaps without changing their heads.

Pose-Prior Corruption

We generate corrupted camera trajectories in metric units before view sampling and normalization. Drift profiles combine sequence-level biases with random motion increments, while the C3VD profile perturbs each pose independently. Each training item uses either the clean trajectory or one of four fixed corrupted versions, consistently across its views.
